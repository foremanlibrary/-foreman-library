✨ WORD #37: MARAMETISM

/marametism/ - Foreman Original

Meaning: Staying present, mastering measurements, doing things in measured intentional doses. Not overdoing, not underdoing - just enough with awareness.

Example: "I felt marametism today - I worked measured, rested measured, loved measured."
